# Quantum Sand Tonat

In Latin, tonat means “it thunders”.

Tonat is a real-time geospatial event notifications feed.

Example scenarios;

* IoT wildfire detection; smart sensors and networks to find forest fires early.
* IoT mudslide and landslide detection; smart field sensors and networks to measure slope stability in real-time.
* IoT poaching detection; smart sensors, AI vision, and wireless networks to spot illegal hunters in real-time.
* IoT forest health; smart sensors measure tree growth, soil moisture, and trunk movement to safeguard forests.
* IoT water and air quality; smart sensors and aquatic probes track pollution levels and oxygen drops in real-time to protect local ecosystems.
* IoT species tracking; smart sensor tags monitor the health, vital signs, and migration routes of endangered animals.
